# Сгенерированные аватары

Четыре вымышленных клиента. Созданы встроенным ImageGen (built-in), по одному вызову на портрет. Изображения сохранены в public/avatars и загружаются локально без внешних сервисов.

## anna

Файл: `public/avatars/anna.png`.

Промпт:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE premium illustrated person avatar for a Russian CRM client list. A fictional woman about 30 years old, warm light skin, shoulder length wavy chestnut hair tucked behind one ear, subtle smile, sage-green crew neck sweater, soft warm peach background. Consistent series art direction: beautifully crafted soft 3D editorial clay-style portrait, sophisticated restrained materials, rounded natural facial features, believable adult proportions, very soft studio lighting, slightly matte tactile surfaces, contemporary premium productivity app aesthetic. Head and upper shoulders centered, straight-on eye contact, generous hair margin, face occupies about 60 percent of the frame and remains recognizable as a 48px circle. Square composition, full opaque solid pastel background edge to edge. Exactly one character. No text, no letters, no logos, no border, no objects, no collage. Not a real person, no celebrity resemblance. High-quality finished UI asset.
